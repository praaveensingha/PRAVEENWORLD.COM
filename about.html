🌍 Meet Praveen Singh – Founder & CEO, PRAVEEN WORLD

NLP & Subconscious Mind Coach • Author • Speaker • Personal Development & Holistic Wellness Coach

My work brings together different approaches to help people explore the subconscious mind,  patterns, emotions, behaviour, presence and holistic wellbeing.

At Praveen World, I work with individuals, professionals, entrepreneurs, students and organizations through NLP • Hypnotherapy • Javanese Magnetism • Mesmerism • Energy Healing • Emotional Release • Holistic Wellness • Personal Development

My intention is simple:
Work with the mind. Understand the emotions. Reconnect with yourself.

🔹 🔹 MY JOURNEY — FROM TECHNOLOGY TO HUMAN POTENTIAL

I started my professional journey in the IT world with a Bachelor's Degree in Information Technology and a Master's Degree in Philosophy.

My early professional experience gave me the opportunity to work with people, teams, leadership and professional development.

By 2008, I was leading a team of over 5,000 professionals.

But as my professional journey continued, another question became increasingly important to me:

Why do people sometimes know what they want to change, yet continue returning to the same patterns?

The more I explored this question, the more interested I became in the relationship between thoughts, language, behaviour, emotions, beliefs and subconscious processes.

That exploration eventually led me toward NLP, personal development and holistic approaches to wellbeing.

🔹 WHY I BUILT PRAVEEN WORLD

I created Praveen World to bring different approaches to personal development and holistic wellbeing together in one place.

People are not just their goals, careers or achievements.

There are also:

Thoughts.
Beliefs.
Emotions.
Learned patterns.
Responses.
Relationships.
Inner experiences.

Sometimes meaningful change begins by understanding these layers more clearly.

That is the space I aim to create through my work.

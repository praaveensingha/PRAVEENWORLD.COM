Privacy Policy – Praveenworld.com

Last Updated: April 1, 2026

Praveen World (“Praveen World”, “we”, “us”, or “our”), operated by Praveen Singh, respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide to us.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect and disclose personal information when you visit praveenworld.com, contact us, book or purchase our services, or otherwise interact with Praveen World.

Where applicable, we will obtain consent or rely on another lawful basis for processing personal information in accordance with applicable law.

1. SCOPE OF THIS POLICY
This Privacy Policy applies to:

praveenworld.com
Forms and enquiry submissions on our website
Sessions, programs, courses and workshops offered by Praveen World
Online and in-person services
Communication between you and Praveen World
Third-party platforms used by us to provide or manage our services

This Privacy Policy applies to Praveen World and does not cover separate businesses, websites or services that are not operated as part of Praveen World.

2. INFORMATION WE MAY COLLECT
Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect the following information:

Personal Information
Full name
Email address
Phone number
City, State or Country
Information you provide when making an enquiry
Information necessary to arrange or provide a service
Booking or session-related information

Payment Information

Where you purchase a service, payment may be processed through Razorpay, our third-party payment service provider.

We generally do not store your complete payment-card, banking or payment credentials on our own systems. Payment information may be collected and processed by Razorpay in accordance with its own privacy policy, terms and security practices.

Praveen World may receive limited transaction-related information necessary to confirm and manage your payment, booking or purchase.

Technical and Usage Information

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically, which may include:

IP address
Browser type and version
Device type
Operating system
Pages visited
Approximate usage information
Referring website or source
Cookies and similar technologies

3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION
We may use personal information to:

Respond to enquiries and requests
Schedule and manage sessions
Provide purchased services
Manage registrations and bookings
Process payments through authorized payment providers
Communicate service-related information
Send appointment or session reminders
Provide customer support
Improve our website, services and user experience
Maintain website security
Prevent fraud, misuse or unauthorized activity
Comply with applicable legal obligations
Send educational or promotional communications where appropriate consent has been obtained

We use personal information only for legitimate and relevant purposes connected with our services and operations.

4. COOKIES AND TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES
Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to:

Enable essential website functionality
Remember preferences
Understand website usage
Improve website performance
Analyze traffic and user behaviour

You may manage or disable cookies through your browser settings.

Some website features may not function properly if certain cookies are disabled.

Where third-party analytics or advertising technologies are used, their own privacy policies may also apply.

5. COMMUNICATIONS
When you submit your contact details or communicate with us, we may contact you regarding:

Your enquiry
Bookings and sessions
Payments
Service-related information
Changes or updates relating to a service
Customer support

Where promotional communications require consent, appropriate consent will be obtained.

You may withdraw your consent for promotional communications or opt out at any time by contacting:

help@praveenworld.com

Where an unsubscribe mechanism is provided, you may also use that mechanism.

6. SHARING OF PERSONAL INFORMATION
We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

We may share limited personal information with trusted third parties where reasonably necessary to operate our website or provide our services, including:

Website and hosting providers
Razorpay, our authorized payment service provider
Booking and scheduling platforms
Learning or course platforms
CRM and communication tools
Email, WhatsApp or messaging service providers
Webinar or online-session platforms
Technical and analytics providers
Professional advisers where necessary
Government, regulatory or law-enforcement authorities where required by applicable law

Third-party service providers may process personal information according to their own terms, privacy policies and security practices.

We seek to share only the information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

7. DATA RETENTION
We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including:

Providing and managing services
Maintaining business and transaction records
Resolving disputes
Maintaining security
Meeting applicable legal, tax, accounting or regulatory requirements

When personal information is no longer reasonably required, we may delete, anonymize or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable legal obligations.

8. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS
Subject to applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the ability to:

Request access to information we hold about you
Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
Request deletion where legally applicable
Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
Request information about the processing of your personal data, where such information is required to be provided under applicable law
Raise a privacy-related grievance
Exercise other rights available to you under applicable law

To exercise a privacy-related right or raise a concern, contact:

help@praveenworld.com

We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests.

We will respond to privacy-related requests and grievances within the period required by applicable law.

9. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
Our services are intended for individuals 18 years of age or older.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from individuals under 18 through our services.

If we become aware that personal information relating to a minor has been collected without appropriate authorization, we may take reasonable steps to delete or address such information, subject to applicable law.

10. THIRD-PARTY LINKS
Our website may contain links to third-party websites, platforms or services.

These third parties operate independently and may have their own privacy policies and terms.

Praveen World is not responsible for the privacy practices, security, content or policies of third-party websites.

We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any third-party service before providing personal information to it.

11. SECURITY
We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, loss, alteration or disclosure.

However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You acknowledge that internet-based services carry inherent security risks.

12. DATA BREACHES AND SECURITY INCIDENTS
If we become aware of a personal-data breach affecting information processed by us and applicable law requires notification, we will take appropriate steps in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

Where required, we may notify affected individuals and/or relevant authorities through appropriate channels.

We will maintain appropriate records and take reasonable steps to contain, assess and address applicable personal-data breaches.

13. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in:

Our services
Our website
Technology
Data-processing practices
Applicable laws or regulations

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised Last Updated date.

You should review this Privacy Policy periodically.

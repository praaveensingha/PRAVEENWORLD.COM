❓FAQ – Frequently Asked Questions 

Have questions about Praveen World? Find answers to help you understand our services and approach.
1. What makes the Praveen World approach different?

Praveen World brings together modern coaching methods, traditional spiritual practices and holistic wellness approaches. The focus is on understanding individual interests and discussing suitable options.

2. Do I need to know which program I want before booking?

No. You can begin with a free discovery call to discuss your interests and understand the available approaches.

3. Are all programs suitable for everyone?

No. Suitability depends on individual needs, preferences and the nature of the practice. We discuss this before proceeding.

4. Can I choose between online and in-person sessions?

Yes. Online sessions are available, and in-person sessions can be explored in Moradabad, depending on the program.

5. Do you offer individual sessions?

Individual sessions are available. Group coaching may also be explored depending on the program and schedule.

6. How long does the transformation journey take?

There is no fixed timeline. Personal experiences and progress vary from one individual to another.

7. Do you guarantee results?

No. We do not guarantee specific outcomes. Our focus is on personal awareness, learning and development.

8. Are your wellness practices a substitute for medical treatment?

No. Coaching and spiritual wellness practices are complementary and are not substitutes for medical care or qualified mental health treatment.

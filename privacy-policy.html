1. Introduction & Scope

Welcome to Praveen World (praveenworld.com), operated by Praveen Singh. We are committed to safeguarding your personal data, privacy, and confidentiality across all our coaching programs and services.

This Privacy Policy applies to personal information collected through our official website (praveenworld.com), inquiry and consultation forms, transformational coaching sessions, holistic training programs, online and in-person consultations, and related communication or third-party digital platforms.

By accessing our website, submitting an inquiry, or booking a session, you acknowledge and agree to the data collection and practices described in this policy.

4. Information Sharing & Third-Party Partners

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We share data only with verified service providers who assist us in operating our services under confidentiality obligations:

Payment & Cloud Infrastructure

Razorpay for secure payment gateway processing; reliable web hosting and secure cloud infrastructure providers.

Communication & Virtual Meetings

Zoom and Google Meet for private online sessions; email service providers and WhatsApp Business for operational notifications.

We may disclose information if strictly required by law, subpoena, or to protect the safety, rights, and security of our clients, Praveen World, or others.

5. Data Retention & Security Protections

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, manage our client relationships, and satisfy statutory accounting, tax, or legal requirements.

We take appropriate administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal details against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. Online communications and web transmissions are protected with industry-standard TLS/SSL encryption.

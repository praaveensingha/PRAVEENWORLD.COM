❓FAQ – Frequently Asked Questions 

Have questions about Praveen World? Find answers to help you understand our services and approach.
1. What is Energy Healing?

Energy Healing is a complementary spiritual and wellness practice based on traditional concepts of energy, intention and balance.

2. Is Energy Healing a medical treatment?

No. Energy Healing at Praveen World is offered as a complementary spiritual and wellness practice. It is not a medical treatment or substitute for professional healthcare.

3. What happens during an in-person session?

The session format is explained beforehand. Depending on the practice, it may involve hands-on or hands-above-the-body techniques, always with your consent and comfort.

4. Is Distance Healing available?

Yes. Distance Healing sessions may be available online. The format and process will be discussed before booking.

5. Do I need to follow a particular religion or belief system?

You do not need to follow a particular religion. You should simply feel comfortable exploring the spiritual or wellness approach being offered.

6. Will I experience the same results as someone else?

Not necessarily. Individual experiences differ, and no particular physical, emotional or spiritual outcome is guaranteed.

7. Can Energy Healing replace medicines or medical treatment?

No. Never stop or change prescribed treatment based on an energy healing session. Continue consulting your qualified healthcare professional.

8. How can I book a session?

You can begin with a free discovery call to discuss your interests, understand the available session formats and ask questions before booking.

Ilmu Gendam is a term associated with traditional Indonesian spiritual and esoteric practices. It is often discussed in connection with ideas of personal influence, presence, suggestion and traditional spiritual knowledge.

At Praveen World, the subject is approached as a traditional area of spiritual exploration, with an emphasis on self-awareness, ethical intention and respect for individual freedom.

❓FAQ – Frequently Asked Questions 

Have questions about Praveen World? Find answers to help you understand our services and approach.
1. What is Ilmu Gendam?

Ilmu Gendam is a term associated with traditional Indonesian spiritual and esoteric practices. Its interpretations and methods may vary across traditions.

2. Is Ilmu Gendam a traditional Indonesian practice?

It is associated with Indonesian traditional spiritual knowledge. Specific beliefs and descriptions differ among practitioners and communities.

3. Can Ilmu Gendam control another person's mind?

Praveen World does not promote mind control, coercion or manipulation. Any personal or spiritual practice should respect the freedom and consent of others.

4. Is Ilmu Gendam the same as Mesmerism?

No. They have different traditional and historical backgrounds, although some descriptions may discuss overlapping themes such as presence or suggestion.

5. Can I explore Ilmu Gendam online?

Online consultation or learning options may be available depending on the nature of the session. Please discuss the format before booking.

6. Do I need previous experience?

No previous experience is necessary for an introductory conversation about the traditional practice.

7. Is Ilmu Gendam a medical treatment?

No. It is presented as a traditional spiritual subject, not a medical treatment or substitute for professional healthcare.

8. Are supernatural results guaranteed?

No. We do not promise supernatural abilities, control over others or guaranteed outcomes.

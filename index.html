❓ Frequently Asked Questions 

FAQ

1. What is NLP?

NLP (Neuro-Linguistic Programming) is an approach that explores the relationship between thoughts, language, perceptions and behavioural patterns. At Praveen World, NLP is used as a tool for self-awareness, mindset exploration, communication and personal development.

2. What is Hypnotherapy?

Hypnotherapy uses guided relaxation and focused attention to create a state of heightened awareness. At Praveen World, it is explored as an approach for personal development, inner exploration and working with patterns, habits and responses.

3. What is Javanese Magnetism?

Javanese Magnetism refers to traditional magnetism-oriented practices associated with personal presence, intention, energetic awareness and holistic traditions. At Praveen World, it is explored as part of a broader personal-development and wellness experience.

4. What is Mesmerism?

Mesmerism is a historical tradition associated with Franz Anton Mesmer and the concept of “animal magnetism.” At Praveen World, Mesmerism is approached in its historical and experiential context, involving focused attention, presence, suggestion and magnetism-oriented practices

5. What is Energy Healing?

Energy Healing is offered as a complementary holistic wellness experience based on traditional beliefs about subtle energy, with a focus on relaxation, grounding, awareness and inner balance. Individual experiences may vary.

6. What is Emotional Release?

Emotional Release is a guided process of becoming more aware of emotional experiences, understanding emotional patterns and creating space to explore and process them.

7. How do I know which approach is right for me?

You don't need to know which approach is right for you before your first conversation.

We begin by understanding what you'd like to explore, your goals and your preferences. From there, we can explore which approach or combination of approaches may be appropriate for your journey.

8. Can I combine different approaches?

Yes. Depending on your goals and circumstances, a journey may include more than one approach—for example, NLP and Hypnotherapy, or personal-development work alongside Emotional Release or holistic wellness practices.

The approach is based on your goals, preferences and the nature of the experience.

9. Are sessions available online and offline?

Sessions may be available online and/or in person, depending on the service and location.

Contact us to know the current availability and session format.

10. How many sessions will I need?

There is no fixed number of sessions that applies to everyone.

Every person's goals, experiences and responses are different. The number and frequency of sessions can be discussed based on your individual journey.

11. What can I expect from a session?

A session generally begins with understanding your goals and what you'd like to explore.

Depending on the approach, the session may involve conversation, guided exercises, focused attention, relaxation, awareness practices or other experiential techniques.

You remain an active participant throughout the process.

12. Is this medical or psychological treatment?

Praveen World provides personal-development, coaching and holistic wellness experiences.

These services are not a substitute for professional healthcare and are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical or mental-health condition.

If you have a medical or mental-health condition or require clinical care, please consult an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.

Individual experiences may vary, and no specific outcome is guaranteed.

13. Will I get guaranteed results?

No.

Every person's experience is different, and outcomes can depend on many individual factors. We do not promise or guarantee a specific result.

Our intention is to provide a supportive space, appropriate tools and an approach that can help you explore meaningful personal development.

14. How do I get started?

You don't need to have everything figured out.

Simply share what you'd like to explore and what you'd like to develop or change.

We'll help you identify an appropriate starting point.

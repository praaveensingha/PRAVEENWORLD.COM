❓ Frequently Asked Questions 

FAQ

1. What is NLP?

NLP (Neuro-Linguistic Programming) is an approach that explores the relationship between thoughts, language, perceptions and behavioural patterns. At Praveen World, NLP is used as a tool for self-awareness, mindset exploration, communication and personal development.

2. What is Hypnotherapy?

Hypnotherapy uses guided relaxation and focused attention to support deeper personal exploration and change. It may be used to work with habits, confidence, emotional patterns, relaxation and other personal-development goals.

3. What is Javanese Magnetism?

Javanese Magnetism refers to traditional magnetism-oriented practices associated with personal presence, intention, energetic awareness and holistic traditions. At Praveen World, it is explored as part of a broader personal-development and wellness experience.

4. What is Mesmerism?

Mesmerism is a historical tradition associated with the concept of “animal magnetism.” At Praveen World, Mesmerism is approached as an experiential practice involving focused attention, presence, suggestion and magnetism-oriented practices.

5. What is Energy Healing?

Energy Healing is offered as a complementary holistic wellness experience focused on relaxation, grounding, awareness and inner balance. Experiences can vary from person to person.

6. What is Emotional Release?

Emotional Release is a guided process of becoming more aware of emotional experiences, understanding them and creating space to move through them in a healthier way.

7. How do I know which approach is right for me?

You don't need to know which approach you need before your first conversation.

We begin by understanding what you're experiencing, what you want to change and what you hope to achieve. From there, we can explore which approach or combination of approaches may be appropriate for your journey.

8. Can I combine different approaches?

Yes. Depending on your goals and circumstances, a journey may include more than one approach—for example, NLP and Hypnotherapy, or personal-development work alongside Emotional Release or holistic wellness practices.

The approach is always based on the individual's needs and goals.

9. Are sessions available online and offline?

Sessions may be available online and/or in person, depending on the service and location.
Contact us to know the current availability and session format.

10. How many sessions will I need?

There is no fixed number of sessions that applies to everyone.

Every person's goals, experiences and responses are different. The number and frequency of sessions can be discussed based on your individual journey.

11. What can I expect from a session?

A session generally begins with understanding your goals and current experience.
Depending on the approach, the session may involve conversation, guided exercises, focused attention, relaxation, awareness practices or other experiential techniques.

You remain an active participant throughout the process.

12. Is this medical or psychological treatment?

Praveen World provides coaching, personal-development and holistic wellness services.

These services are not a substitute for medical treatment, psychiatric care or psychological therapy. If you have a medical or mental-health condition, please consult an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.

13. Will I get guaranteed results?

No.
Every person's experience is different, and outcomes can depend on many individual factors. We do not promise or guarantee a specific result.

Our intention is to provide a supportive space, appropriate tools and an approach that can help you explore meaningful personal change.

14. How do I get started?

You don't need to have everything figured out.
Simply share what you're experiencing and what you'd like to change.
We'll help you identify an appropriate starting point.

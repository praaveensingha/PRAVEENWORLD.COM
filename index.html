❓FAQ – Frequently Asked Questions 

Have questions about Praveen World? Find answers to help you understand our services and approach.
1. What is Praveen World?

Praveen World is a personal transformation and holistic wellness platform founded by Coach Praveen Singh. It brings together NLP Coaching, Subconscious Mind Coaching, Hypnotherapy, Emotional Wellness, Energy Healing and traditional Javanese practices.

2. What is NLP Coaching?

NLP (Neuro-Linguistic Programming) is an approach that explores the relationship between thoughts, language, behaviour and learned patterns. NLP Coaching helps individuals explore their thinking patterns, communication, confidence and personal goals.

3. What is Subconscious Mind Coaching?

Subconscious Mind Coaching focuses on exploring beliefs, habits and learned responses that may influence everyday experiences. It supports self-awareness and personal development.

4. What is Hypnotherapy?

Hypnotherapy uses focused attention and relaxation as part of a guided process. At Praveen World, it is offered as an approach for personal exploration and development.

5. Are online sessions available?

Yes. Online sessions are available through suitable online arrangements, allowing people to connect with Praveen World from different locations.

6. What is Emotional Release?

Emotional Release is a guided process of becoming more aware of emotional experiences, understanding emotional patterns and creating space to explore and process them.

7. What is Energy Healing?

Energy Healing is offered as a complementary spiritual and wellness practice. Individual experiences vary, and it is not a substitute for medical diagnosis or treatment.

8. What is Javanese Magnetism?

Javanese Magnetism is presented at Praveen World as a traditional Javanese energy-based practice. Interested individuals can enquire to understand the approach and session details.

9. What is Mesmerism?

Mesmerism is a historical practice associated with focused attention and trance-related traditions. Praveen World introduces it as part of its traditional knowledge offerings.

10. What is Ilmu Gendam?

Ilmu Gendam is presented as a traditional practice explored in its cultural and historical context, with an emphasis on ethical and responsible understanding.

11. How do I choose the right service?

You can begin with a Free Discovery Call. We can understand your interests and discuss which available approach may be relevant to your goals.

12. What happens during a Free Discovery Call?

It is an introductory conversation to understand what you are looking for, answer your questions and explore possible next steps. It does not guarantee a particular outcome.

13. Are results guaranteed?

No. Every individual's circumstances and experiences are different. Praveen World does not guarantee specific outcomes. Coaching and complementary wellness practices do not replace appropriate professional healthcare.

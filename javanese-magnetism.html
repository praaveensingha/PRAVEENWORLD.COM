Understanding Javanese Magnetism

Javanese Magnetism is a traditional energy-based practice associated with Javanese spiritual traditions.

It is commonly discussed in connection with concepts such as vital force, personal presence, inner awareness and spiritual intention.

At Praveen World, this practice is presented as a complementary spiritual and wellness experience. It offers an opportunity to explore traditional perspectives on energy, awareness and personal development.

Key Ideas Associated With the Practice:
- Traditional concepts of vital energy.
- Inner awareness and personal presence.
- Spiritual intention and focused attention.
- Personal exploration and reflection.
- Respect for traditional spiritual practices.

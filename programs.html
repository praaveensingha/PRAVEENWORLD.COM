THREE PILLARS OF PRAVEEN WORLD

Three Pillars. Multiple Approaches. One Journey of Transformation.

Every individual is different. Some people want to understand their thoughts and subconscious patterns. Some are curious about traditional wisdom and spiritual practices. Others seek emotional balance, holistic wellness and personal growth.

At Praveen World, we bring together three complementary pillars to help individuals explore themselves, understand their challenges and discover new possibilities.

Our approach combines modern mind-development methods, traditional Javanese practices and holistic wellness approaches.
Modern Mind Transformation

Understand Your Mind. Transform Your Patterns. Discover New Possibilities.

Your thoughts, beliefs, emotions and learned behaviours influence how you experience everyday life.

Sometimes, you consciously want to change a habit, develop confidence or move forward, but familiar patterns continue to influence your responses.

Mind Transformation focuses on understanding these patterns and exploring more helpful ways of thinking, responding and behaving.

Our Areas of Expertise

1. NLP Coaching

Explore the connection between thoughts, language, beliefs and behaviour. NLP Coaching provides approaches for developing self-awareness, communication, confidence, goal clarity and personal development.

2. Subconscious Mind Coaching

Explore learned beliefs, habits and recurring responses that may influence everyday decisions and experiences. Develop greater awareness of your inner patterns and explore new perspectives.

3. Hypnotherapy

A guided approach involving focused attention and relaxation. It offers an opportunity for personal exploration and working with thoughts, feelings and patterns.

4. Emotional Release

Understand emotional responses, recurring patterns and personal experiences. Explore supportive approaches to emotional awareness and wellbeing.

Ancient Javanese Wisdom

Discover Traditional Knowledge. Explore Ancient Practices.

Across generations, different cultures have developed traditional practices connected with spirituality, inner awareness and human experience.

Praveen World also explores traditional Javanese knowledge through selected practices, presented with respect for their cultural context and responsible application.

Our Areas of Expertise

1. Javanese Magnetism

Explore a traditional Javanese energy-based practice associated with vital force, spiritual traditions and personal exploration.

2. Mesmerism

Discover a historical practice associated with focused attention, suggestion and trance-related traditions. Explore its background and how it is approached at Praveen World.

3. Ilmu Gendam

Explore traditional knowledge and its cultural context through a responsible and ethical perspective. The focus is on learning and understanding, not manipulation or control of others.

Our Philosophy

Traditional knowledge deserves thoughtful exploration, cultural respect and ethical responsibility. We aim to present these practices transparently without promising supernatural powers or guaranteed results.

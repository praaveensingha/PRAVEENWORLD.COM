FAQ

What is NLP?

NLP, or Neuro-Linguistic Programming, is a personal-development approach that explores the relationship between language, patterns of thinking and behaviour.

What is Hypnotherapy?

Hypnotherapy involves guided relaxation and focused attention and may be explored as an approach for inner exploration, habits, perceptions and personal-development goals.

What is Javanese Magnetism?

Javanese Magnetism refers to traditional magnetism-oriented practices associated with personal presence, intention and energetic awareness, explored within a holistic personal-development setting.

What is Mesmerism?

Mesmerism is a historical tradition associated with Franz Anton Mesmer and the concept of “animal magnetism.” At Praveen World, it is explored in an experiential context through focused attention, presence, suggestion and magnetism-oriented practices.

What is Emotional Release?

Emotional Release provides a guided space for exploring emotional experiences, patterns and personal awareness.

What is Energy Healing?

Energy Healing is offered as a complementary holistic wellness practice based on traditional beliefs about subtle energy, with a focus on relaxation, grounding, awareness and personal wellbeing.

Do I need to choose one approach?

Not necessarily. The appropriate approach depends on your goals, preferences and the nature of the experience.

Are these services medical treatments?

No. Praveen World provides personal-development, coaching and holistic wellness services. These services are not a substitute for professional healthcare and are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical or mental-health condition.

Are results guaranteed?

No. Individual experiences and outcomes vary from person to person. No specific result is guaranteed.

Can organizations book a corporate program?

Yes. Customized corporate and professional-development programs may be available for organizations, teams and professionals.

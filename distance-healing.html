What Is Distance Healing?

Understanding Distance Healing

Distance Healing is a complementary spiritual and energy-based practice in which the practitioner and participant are not physically present in the same location.

Some traditional approaches understand this practice through ideas of energy, intention and spiritual connection.

At Praveen World, Distance Healing is offered as a spiritual and wellness experience for personal exploration and reflection.

It does not involve a physical examination or medical treatment, and individual experiences may vary.

What Makes It Different?
- You can participate from your preferred private space.
- No travel to Moradabad is required.
- The session can be discussed and arranged online.
- The practice is approached with respect for your comfort and beliefs.
- You can reflect on your experience afterwards.

Distance Healing & In-Person Energy Healing

Two Ways to Explore Energy Healing

Both formats offer an opportunity to explore the same broader spiritual and wellness interests, but the experience and setting are different.

Distance Healing

Practitioner and participant are in different locations.

Session is arranged remotely.

No travel is required.

The practice is conducted through an agreed online format.

In-Person Energy Healing

Practitioner and participant meet in person.

Session takes place in Moradabad.

You visit the session location.

Depending on the practice, hands-on or hands-above-the-body techniques may be used with consent.

You can discuss both options with Praveen World and decide which format is more suitable for your preferences and circumstances.

❓FAQ – Frequently Asked Questions 

Have questions about Praveen World? Find answers to help you understand our services and approach.
1. What is Distance Healing?

Distance Healing is a complementary spiritual and energy-based wellness practice explored remotely when the practitioner and participant are in different locations.

2. Does Distance Healing require physical presence?

No. The session is arranged remotely, so you can participate from your preferred private space.

3. Can I book Distance Healing from anywhere in India?

Yes. Online sessions may be available for participants across India, subject to scheduling and availability.

4. Can people living outside India participate?

Online sessions may be arranged for international participants, subject to time-zone coordination and availability.

5. Do I need to join a video call?

The format depends on the agreed session process. You will be informed about the requirements before booking.

6. What should I do during a Distance Healing session?

Follow the instructions shared before the session. Usually, you should arrange a quiet and comfortable space where you can participate without interruptions.

7. Can Distance Healing cure physical or mental health conditions?

No. Distance Healing is offered as a complementary spiritual and wellness practice, not as a medical treatment. It should not replace professional healthcare or prescribed treatment.

8. Will everyone experience the same thing?

No. Individual experiences differ. Some people may find the experience meaningful, while others may have different or no noticeable experiences. No specific outcome is guaranteed.

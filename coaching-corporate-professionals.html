❓FAQ – Frequently Asked Questions 

Have questions about Praveen World? Find answers to help you understand our services and approach.
1. What is mindset coaching for corporate professionals?

It is a personal-development approach that helps professionals explore their thinking patterns, beliefs, confidence, communication and personal goals.

2. Can NLP Coaching help me improve my confidence?

NLP Coaching can provide opportunities to explore self-beliefs, communication patterns and personal strategies related to confidence. Individual experiences vary.

3. Can coaching help with workplace stress?

Coaching can help you reflect on your responses, priorities and personal strategies. It is not a substitute for professional mental health treatment.

4. Do I need to leave my job to attend coaching?

No. Online sessions can be scheduled by mutual agreement around your availability.

5. Are sessions available online?

Yes. Online coaching sessions are available across India and may also be arranged internationally, subject to scheduling.

6. How many sessions will I need?

There is no fixed number. The number of sessions depends on your goals, preferences and individual progress.

7. Is coaching confidential?

Your personal discussions should be handled respectfully and with appropriate privacy. Confidentiality arrangements and their limits can be discussed before beginning.

8. Are career growth or promotion results guaranteed?

No. Coaching does not guarantee promotion, salary increases or career success. It focuses on personal awareness, reflection and development.

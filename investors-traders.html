Revenge Trading & The Loss Spiral

Triggered by the gambler's fallacy and bruised ego after an initial red trade. You impulsively double contract sizing, break position limits, and experience emotional tilt that annihilates weeks of methodical discipline in 30 minutes.

emergency Root Cause: Subconscious conflation of capital loss with personal worth and public status.
electric_bolt FOMO & Chasing Green Candles

Watching a parabolic breakout trigger dopaminergic greed. You jump into high-volatility momentum without trade setup validation or calculated risk-to-reward parameters, buying at the absolute peak before distribution wipes you out.

emergency Root Cause: Scarcity programming and compulsive fear of being excluded from social abundance.
hourglass_disabled Premature Profit Exits & Holding Bleeding Reds

You scalp tiny 5-point greens out of anxiety while 'hoping and praying' on catastrophic red positions, widening stop-losses as prices crash downward. Loss aversion dictates your order book, turning a 1:3 strategy into an inverted trap.

emergency Root Cause: Primitive amygdala avoidance of cognitive pain, overruling rational mathematical expectation.
heart_broken Heart Palpitations & Screen Fatigue

Sweaty palms, elevated pulse, shallow breathing, and six straight hours of continuous hypnotic tick-gazing. This adrenaline-drenched survival posture exhausts your prefrontal cortex, precipitating catastrophic afternoon misclicks.

emergency Root Cause: Dysregulated sympathetic nervous system operating under existential fight-or-flight threat.

Refund, Return & Cancellation Policy – Praveenworld.com

Last Updated: April 1, 2026

At Praveen World, we provide personal-development coaching, NLP, Hypnotherapy, Javanese Magnetism, Mesmerism, Emotional Release, Energy Healing, workshops, courses and related educational and holistic wellness services.

This Refund, Return & Cancellation Policy applies to purchases and bookings made through praveenworld.com.

1. NO-REFUND POLICY

All payments made for sessions, programs, courses, workshops and other services offered by Praveen World are non-refundable once payment has been completed, subject to any rights that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited under applicable law.

By completing a purchase or booking, you acknowledge and agree to this No-Refund Policy.

2. CANCELLATIONS

Once payment has been completed and a booking, session, program, course or service has been confirmed, the payment is non-refundable.

Cancellation by the customer does not create an entitlement to a refund.

This applies regardless of the reason for cancellation, including:

Change of mind
Change in personal circumstances
Change in personal goals or preferences
Failure to attend a scheduled session
Failure to use or complete a course or program
Personal scheduling conflicts
Failure to achieve an expected or desired outcome

3. RESCHEDULING

Where a session-based service permits rescheduling, a request may be considered subject to availability and the applicable service conditions.

Rescheduling is not guaranteed and does not create a right to a refund.

Any rescheduling arrangement must be confirmed by Praveen World in writing.

4. NON-ATTENDANCE AND NO-SHOWS

If you do not attend a scheduled session, workshop, appointment or other service without an approved rescheduling arrangement, the payment remains non-refundable.

Failure to attend does not create an automatic right to reschedule or receive a refund.

5. DIGITAL PRODUCTS, COURSES AND PROGRAMS

Payments for digital products, online courses, downloadable materials, recorded content, training programs or other digital services are non-refundable once payment has been completed or access has been provided, subject to applicable law.

You are responsible for ensuring that you have reviewed the service description and requirements before purchasing.

6. TECHNICAL ACCESS ISSUES

If you experience a genuine technical problem that prevents you from accessing a paid digital service or course, please contact us at:

help@praveenworld.com

We will make reasonable efforts to investigate and resolve genuine technical access issues.

Technical support or restoration of access does not automatically create a right to a refund.

7. SERVICE OUTCOMES

Payment is for the service, session, course, program or experience provided by Praveen World and not for a guaranteed result.

Individual experiences and outcomes may vary.

No refund will be provided solely because a customer does not experience a particular personal, emotional, professional, wellness or other desired outcome.

8. EXCEPTIONS REQUIRED BY LAW

Nothing in this Policy is intended to exclude or limit any refund, cancellation, replacement, remedy or other consumer right that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited under applicable law.

Where a refund or other remedy is legally required, it will be handled in accordance with the applicable law.

9. THIRD-PARTY PAYMENT PROCESSING

Payments may be processed through Razorpay, our authorized third-party payment service provider.

Payment processing may be subject to Razorpay's applicable terms, policies and procedures.

Where a payment-related issue occurs, we may work with the payment provider to investigate and resolve the matter.

10. POLICY UPDATES

We may update this Refund, Return & Cancellation Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, business practices or applicable legal requirements.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised Last Updated date.

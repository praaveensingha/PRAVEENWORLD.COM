Detailed Policy Inquiries

block Can I request a refund if I change my mind or my personal goals change?

No. Cancellation by the customer does not create an entitlement to a refund. This includes change of mind, revised personal goals, personal circumstances, failure to attend or complete, scheduling conflicts, or lack of expected individual results.

trending_up Are specific results or transformation outcomes guaranteed?

No. Payment is made strictly for the professional service and time delivered. Because individual results depend on personal practice, integration, and readiness, no guarantees are made regarding specific subjective outcomes.

cloud_download What if I encounter technical issues accessing a digital product?

Digital courses and products remain strictly non-refundable once access is provided. However, if you experience genuine technical access or downloading problems, write directly to help@praveenworld.com and our team will resolve your access.

credit_card How are payments and processing fees handled?

Payments are processed through secure third-party payment gateways such as Razorpay. Once transaction completion is registered, all terms of this policy apply automatically under applicable statutory frameworks.

Understanding Hypnotherapy

At Praveen World, Coach Praveen Singh offers guided hypnotherapy sessions online worldwide and in-person in Moradabad, India, providing a supportive and respectful space for self-exploration and personal development.

Hypnotherapy is a guided process that may involve focused attention, relaxation, imagination and therapeutic suggestions.

During a session, a practitioner may guide you towards a relaxed and focused state in which you can explore thoughts, feelings and personal experiences.

Hypnosis is not sleep or mind control. You remain a participant in the process and can communicate, respond or stop whenever you wish.

At Praveen World, hypnotherapy is approached with respect, comfort and individual consent.

What to understand about hypnotherapy:
- It is a guided process, not mind control.
- You remain involved and can communicate during the session.
- You are not required to accept suggestions that feel uncomfortable.
- Each person's experience may be different.
- It does not guarantee a particular result.

❓FAQ – Frequently Asked Questions 

Have questions about Praveen World? Find answers to help you understand our services and approach.
1. What is hypnotherapy?

Hypnotherapy is a guided process that may use focused attention, relaxation, imagery and suggestions to support personal exploration and development.

2. Is hypnosis the same as sleep?

No. Hypnosis is generally described as a state of focused attention. It is not ordinary sleep.

3. Can someone control my mind during hypnosis?

No. Hypnosis does not mean surrendering your will or losing control. You remain an active participant.

4. Will I be unconscious during a session?

Not necessarily. Experiences vary, and many people remain aware of their surroundings and the process.

5. Can hypnotherapy help me change habits?

Some people explore hypnotherapy as one approach to understanding habits and supporting personal change. Individual experiences and outcomes vary.

6. Are online hypnotherapy sessions available?

Yes. Online sessions are available through Zoom, subject to suitability and prior discussion.

7. How many sessions will I need?

This depends on your goals, preferences and individual circumstances. We can discuss this during your discovery call.

8. Is hypnotherapy a replacement for medical or psychological treatment?

No. Our sessions are intended for personal development and wellness. They are not a substitute for diagnosis or treatment by qualified healthcare professionals.

Understand the Connection Between Your Mind, Language & Behaviour.

NLP stands for Neuro-Linguistic Programming.

It is an approach that explores how people process experiences, use language, develop patterns of thinking and respond to different situations.

- Neuro: How we process experiences through our senses and nervous system.
- Linguistic: How language influences the way we express and interpret experiences.
- Programming: How learned patterns and habits can influence our responses and behaviour.

NLP Coaching uses these ideas as a framework for self-exploration, communication and personal development.

❓FAQ – Frequently Asked Questions 

Have questions about Praveen World? Find answers to help you understand our services and approach.
1. What is NLP Coaching?

NLP Coaching explores the relationship between thoughts, language, beliefs and behaviour to support self-awareness and personal development.

2. Is NLP Coaching suitable for beginners?

Yes. You do not need previous knowledge of NLP. Sessions can begin with your questions, interests and personal goals.

3. How many NLP sessions will I need?

The number of sessions depends on your individual requirements, goals and preferences. This can be discussed during an introductory conversation.

4. Are online NLP sessions available?

Yes. Online sessions are available for people in different locations, subject to scheduling.

5. Can NLP Coaching help with confidence and overthinking?

These are common areas people may wish to explore through coaching. Individual experiences and results vary.

6. Is NLP Coaching the same as Hypnotherapy?

No. They are different approaches. NLP explores thinking, language and behavioural patterns, while hypnotherapy involves focused attention and relaxation.

7. Does NLP Coaching guarantee results?

No. No specific result is guaranteed. Personal experiences and progress vary.

8. Is NLP Coaching a replacement for mental healthcare?

No. Coaching is not a substitute for medical diagnosis, psychological assessment or mental healthcare.
